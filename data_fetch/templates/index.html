About Williams VIX Fix

Description

The Williams VIX Fix measures market fear and identifies potential market bottoms when fear is high. It uses price action instead of options data.

Interpretation

  • ● High values (green bars) suggest potential buying opportunities
  • ● Range lines show historical percentile levels
  • ● Upper band indicates overbought fear levels
